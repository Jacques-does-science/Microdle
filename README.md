# Microdle

Microdle is a food microbiology word puzzle where players identify a hidden food microbe in eight guesses.

The game focuses on microbes relevant to food safety, food spoilage, and fermentation, including bacterial pathogens, spoilage organisms, yeasts, molds, viruses, and parasites. It is designed as a lightweight educational puzzle for food science students, microbiology learners, fermentation enthusiasts, and food safety professionals.

Play the game here:
https://jacques-does-science.github.io/Microdle/

## Overview

Microdle challenges players to identify a hidden microbe genus from a curated food microbiology database. Each guess provides letter-based feedback:
- Correct letters in the correct position are marked as exact matches.
- Correct letters in the wrong position are marked as partial matches.
- Letters that are not part of the answer are marked as absent.
- Blank trailing positions are also evaluated because microbe names vary in length.

The goal is to identify the hidden microbe within eight attempts.

## Educational Focus

Microdle is intended to make food microbiology more engaging and approachable. The game includes organisms associated with:
- Foodborne illness
- Food spoilage
- Fermentation
- Dairy production
- Brewing and baking
- Fresh produce contamination
- Seafood safety
- Refrigerated food risks
- Probiotic and starter culture organisms

Rather than functioning only as a spelling puzzle, Microdle is designed to reinforce recognition of important food microbes and their biological or food-related significance.
