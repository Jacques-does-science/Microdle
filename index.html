Microdle

What is Microdle?

Microdle is an educational food microbiology game designed as a science-themed microbiology word puzzle. In this interactive microbe identification game, players are challenged to identify a hidden genus of foodborne pathogens, food spoilage organisms, or beneficial fermentation microbes within eight attempts.

The game tests your knowledge of biology, biochemistry, food science, and hygiene. It provides structural feedback for entered words, turning a fun gameplay session into an interactive food science learning game.

Who is Microdle for?

Whether you are a student preparing for a food safety game or test, a culinary enthusiast fascinated by sourdough and cheese, or a scientist who loves a good microbiology word puzzle, Microdle is for you. It bridges academic research and gamified education, providing an accessible yet challenging intellectual workout. Non-experts can use the progressive lab assay hint system to reveal specimen classifications, habitats, and clinical indicators as they play.

What microbes are included?

The game selects from a curated list of microbes that play a vital role in our food supply chain. Players will encounter:

  • Pathogens: Test your skills with a foodborne pathogen quiz covering well-known threats like Salmonella, Listeria, and Escherichia.
  • Spoilage Organisms: Learn about organisms responsible for fresh food deterioration, such as Pseudomonas.
  • Fermentation Microbes: Challenge yourself on a fermentation microbe quiz featuring beneficial yeasts and bacteria like Saccharomyces and Lactobacillus.

How to Play

Identify the hidden food microbe in 8 tries.

Microbes can be pathogens, spoilage organisms, or fermentation organisms. Names vary in length.

V

V is in the microbe and in the correct spot.

I

I is in the microbe but in the wrong spot.

B

B is not in the microbe in any spot.

Stuck? After your 2nd guess, Run Lab Assay unlocks hints one guess at a time: the microbe's class, where it's found, a clue, and finally its first letter.
